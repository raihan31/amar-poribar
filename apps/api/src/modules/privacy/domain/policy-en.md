# Amar Poribar Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-01

## What we collect

- Your phone number and name, and the family you belong to.
- The money entries your family records: amounts, dates, categories, notes and receipts.
- Messages, voice notes, video messages and call records inside your family.
- Technical data needed to run the app, such as device type and app version.

## How we use it

We use your data only to run the app for your family: showing budgets and reports, syncing between devices and sending reminders. We never sell your data.

## AI processing outside Bangladesh

When you use voice entry, receipt reading, the monthly summary or Ask, the text or image you send is processed by an AI service whose servers are outside Bangladesh. We send no names and no full phone numbers. Voice notes and video messages between family members are never sent to AI.

## How long we keep it

| Data | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Deleted transactions <!--soft_deleted--> | 30 days, then purged |
| Voice notes and video messages <!--voice_video--> | 180 days, then deleted |
| Call logs <!--call_logs--> | 90 days |
| Notifications <!--notifications--> | 90 days |
| Expired invite links <!--invites--> | 30 days |
| Used sign-in codes <!--otp--> | 24 hours |

Voice notes are stored for 180 days.

## Your rights

- Export: you can download a copy of your data at any time.
- Erasure: you can ask us to delete your account and data. We complete it within 30 days, and you can cancel by logging in during that time.
